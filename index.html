Hi, my name is Dmitry, or simply Dima.

I work as a Senior Staff AI/ML Engineer in Search Quality at Google Zurich, focusing on Grounding and Factuality of GenAI Search (AI Overviews and AI Mode).

I am interested in building, evaluating and deploying production ML/AI systems in Unverifiable Domains, focusing on Agentic Information Retrieval, Distillation and Evals.

My background in three points:

  • I hold a PhD from ETH Zurich, Institute of Machine Learning. I worked there on optimization techniques for general computer vision and medical imaging under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Joachim M. Buhmann;
  • Prior to that, I earned a diploma in Applied Math and Computer Science from Lomonosov Moscow State University. There I majored in Machine Learning as a part of Bayesian Methods research group headed by Dr. Dmitry P. Vetrov;
  • Alongside my studies, I worked at Yandex as a Search Quality analyst, PwC as an IT Audit Associate.
